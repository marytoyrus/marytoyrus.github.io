claude sheet api

claude sheet api is an internal tool of Mary Toys, a small online toy seller on Russian marketplaces (Ozon, Wildberries, Yandex Market).

Purpose

The app lets the shop owner manage the shop's own working files with the help of an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic), acting only on the owner's instructions:

  • Google Drive — product photos and videos, shipping labels and product certificates in the shop's own folder: upload new files, rename, move between folders, move unneeded files to trash.
  • Google Sheets — read and update the shop's own inventory and orders spreadsheet.

Who uses it

Only the shop owner, with their own Google account. The app is not offered to the public, has no sign-up and no other users.
