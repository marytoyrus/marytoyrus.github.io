Privacy Policy — claude sheet api

This policy describes how claude sheet api, an internal tool of the Mary Toys shop, accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

1. Who uses the app

The app is used only by the owner of the Mary Toys shop, with their own Google account. It has no other users.

2. Google user data the app accesses

  • Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive): files and folders in the shop's working folder — names, sizes, dates and contents of product photos and videos, shipping labels and product certificates.
  • Google Sheets: the shop's own inventory and orders spreadsheet.
  • The email address of the signed-in account, only to confirm it is the shop owner's account.

The app does not access Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

3. How the data is used

Only to carry out the owner's own instructions: upload, rename, move and move to trash the shop's files, and read and update the shop's spreadsheet. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than these tasks.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not sold and is not shared with third parties, except Anthropic, whose AI assistant Claude performs the tasks on the owner's instructions. Processing by Claude is governed by the shop owner's own agreement with Anthropic. No people other than the shop owner read the data.

5. Storage and retention

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive. The app keeps no database and no permanent copies: temporary copies made while a task runs are deleted together with the working environment. The access token is stored only in the owner's private environment settings.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Third-party apps & services → claude sheet api → Delete all connections). After that the app can no longer access any Google data. Since the app stores no copies, nothing else needs to be deleted.

7. Contact

Questions: the support email shown on the Google consent screen of this app.

По-русски

Приложением пользуется только владелец магазина Mary Toys. С его разрешения приложение работает с рабочей папкой магазина в Google Drive (фото и видео товаров, этикетки, сертификаты) и с таблицей учёта в Google Таблицах — только чтобы выполнять его поручения. Данные не продаются и не передаются третьим лицам, кроме Anthropic, чей ИИ-ассистент Claude выполняет поручения владельца. Копий приложение не хранит. Отозвать доступ можно в любой момент: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
